# PAY54 Batch 26 / 1058 — Regulated UK–Nigeria Payment-Rail Acceptance

**Scope:** launch corridor only; no signed provider contract, regulatory licence or production rail is proven in this batch.

PAY54 operates as a customer, orchestration and intelligence layer above regulated financial partners. Demand independent UK inbound, global FX/backbone, and two distinct Nigeria payout adapters with nonidentical operator dependencies. Named candidates require due diligence; an approved technical adapter is not a legally approved fund-transfer provider.

Before a transaction feature appears publicly, record executed contracts, legal permissions and regulatory allocation, required KYC/KYB, AML/sanctions/KYT screening, safeguarding/funds flow, client-money treatment, bank/mobile account issuance eligibility, FX quote TTL and fee disclosure, local payout finality, prefunding, money transmission liability and complaint/chargeback arrangements.

Verify cryptographic webhook signatures, deduplication, idempotent reference mapping, partial payout, FX stale-quote denial, AML/manual review, sanctions block, partner timeout, reconciliation mismatches, refund and secondary-provider failover. Never automatically replay a non-idempotent payout across providers. Monetary ledger posting must come from audited server-confirmed settlement, not a browser success notification.
